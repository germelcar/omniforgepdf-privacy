Privacy Policy

OmniForgePDF (Android app) · Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what information the OmniForgePDF Android app (“the app”) handles, why, and the choices you have. It is operated by Germán Meléndrez Carballo, an individual developer. Contact: germelcar@gmail.com.

1. Your files stay on your device

The app processes PDF files locally on your device. The content of your documents, extracted text, file names, file paths, and any passwords you enter for PDF tools are not sent to us or to any third party. If we add features that upload files in the future, this policy will be updated first, and any upload will be optional and explained before it happens.

2. Crash and error reports (Sentry)

To find and fix crashes, the app sends error reports to Sentry, a crash-reporting service. Reports may include the app version, device model and Android version, the screen you were on and the type of action that led to the error, and technical stack traces. Personal and sensitive fields are removed before anything is sent, and default personal-information collection is turned off. Reports do not include the content of your documents.

Sentry stores this data in the United States. Reports are kept for up to 30 days. Sentry processes the data as a service provider on our behalf.

3. Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We process crash reports on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable and secure.

4. Who we share data with

We share crash reports only with Sentry. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for any purpose not described here.

5. How long we keep data

  • Crash reports in Sentry: up to 30 days.
  • Data stored on your device: remains until you remove the app or clear its data.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Uninstall the app to stop all data collection by the app.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold, by emailing germelcar@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children and students

The app is intended for students and professionals aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Crash reports are sent over encrypted connections. Scrubbing rules remove sensitive fields before data leaves your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit what is collected to reduce risk.

9. Advertising (future version)

The current version of the app does not show ads. We may add ads, and an option to remove them, in a later version. Before any ads are shown, this policy will be updated to describe the advertising provider, the data it collects, and the consent choices available to you.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add or change a service. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. Earlier versions are kept in this repository’s history.

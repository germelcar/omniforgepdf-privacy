Privacy Policy

OmniForgePDF (Android app) · Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what information the OmniForgePDF Android app (“the app”) handles, why, and the choices you have. It is operated by Germán Meléndrez Carballo, individual developer, contact germelcar@gmail.com.

1. Your files stay on your device

The app processes PDF files locally on your device. The content of your documents, extracted text, file names, file paths, and any passwords you enter for PDF tools are not sent to us or to any third party. We may add cloud features in the future. If we do, this policy will be updated first, and any upload will be optional and explained before it happens.

2. Crash and error reports (Sentry)

To find and fix crashes, the app sends error reports to Sentry, a crash-reporting service. Reports may include: the app version, device model and Android version, the screen you were on and the type of action that led to the error, and technical stack traces. Before anything is sent, personal and sensitive fields are removed, and default personal-information collection is turned off. Reports do not include the content of your documents.

Sentry stores this data in the United States. Reports are kept for up to 30 days. Sentry acts as a service provider on our behalf.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads at the bottom of the screen through Google AdMob. AdMob is provided by Google Ireland Limited, or Google LLC outside the EEA. AdMob can collect information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device information, app interaction and approximate location, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

  • Non-personalised ads by default. Unless you choose otherwise, the app requests non-personalised ads. These are not based on a profile of your interests.
  • Personalised ads with consent. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, personalised ads are used only if you agree in the consent form shown by the app. You can change your choice at any time using Privacy options in the app’s Settings.
  • United States. You can choose “Don’t sell or share my data” in the consent message, and you can change this in Privacy options. Some interest-based ads may still appear if other companies have sold or shared your information.

Google’s own use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage your ad settings in your Google account and in your device’s advertising settings.

4. Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable and secure.
  • Personalised advertising: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Non-personalised advertising: our legitimate interest in funding the free app, with limited data use.

5. Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers above (Sentry and Google AdMob). We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for any purpose not described here.

6. How long we keep data

  • Crash reports in Sentry: up to 30 days.
  • Advertising data: as described in Google’s policies.
  • Data stored on your device: remains until you remove the app or clear its data.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Change or withdraw ad consent at any time with Privacy options in the app.
  • Uninstall the app to stop all data collection by the app.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold, by emailing germelcar@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children and students

The app is intended for students and professionals aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Crash reports are sent over encrypted connections. Scrubbing rules remove sensitive fields before data leaves your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit what is collected to reduce risk.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add or change a service. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. Earlier versions are kept in this repository’s history.

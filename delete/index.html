Delete Your Data

OmniForge PDF (Android app) · Developer: Germán Meléndrez Carballo · Last updated: 24 October 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Email germelcar@gmail.com with the subject “OmniForge PDF — data deletion request”.
  2. If you can, mention whether crash reporting was enabled on your device (Settings → Send crash reports).
  3. We will delete the matching data and confirm within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Crash and error reports held in Sentry (stack traces, app version, device model, Android version, and the technical context of the error).
  • Diagnostic data attached to those reports.
  • The random installation identifier generated by Sentry for your installation. It is not linked to your name or Google account, so if reports cannot be tied to you, they are deleted wholesale on expiry anyway.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Crash reports expire automatically in Sentry after 30 days, whether or not you request deletion.
  • Your request email is kept only as long as needed to handle it; a record that the request was completed may be kept for up to 2 years.

The update check’s installation identifier

The app’s update check (Firebase Remote Config) uses a random Firebase installation identifier, which Firebase keeps until it is deleted. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account, so we cannot find it from a deletion request. To stop it from being used, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings; a new installation gets a new identifier. Identifiers that are deleted are removed from Google’s systems within 180 days.

Data that never leaves your device

Your PDF files, images, recent-files list, and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, removes them entirely — no request is needed. Turning off Settings → Send crash reports also deletes any queued reports on the device.
